Executive Summary 
The Self-Righting Automatic Dumpster Cleaner is a patent-pending, disposable, and eco-
friendly cleaning innovation designed to simplify and revolutionize dumpster sanitation. 
The device leverages advanced delivery methods to release cleaning agents that effectively 
break down grime, grease, and odors. With its built-in self-righting technology, a pull-tab 
activation system, and a 5-second backup timer, this product ensures optimal coverage and 
reliable performance. 
This versatile product targets both residential and commercial markets, addressing the 
growing need for cost-effective, hygienic, and efficient solutions to maintaining clean trash 
bins and dumpsters. By introducing this innovation to retail, online, and wholesale markets, 
the Self-Righting Automatic Dumpster Cleaner aims to redefine sanitation standards 
nationwide.
