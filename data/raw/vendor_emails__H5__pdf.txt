description
We are developing a one-time-use aerosol-based cleaning device designed
for interior surface coverage in medium-to-large containers. The
requirements for the spray system are:
Vertical spray reach of approx. 45 inches.
360° dispersion at a 45–60° angle to achieve broad internal coverage.
Actuation will be handled by a custom housing system (impact-triggered or
manual delayed release), not the nozzle itself.
Housing is intended to be scalable across can sizes, so can/nozzle
performance consistency is the main requirement.
Open to recommendations on can dimensions and nozzle assemblies that
can meet these performance criteria.
 
start
As soon as possible. We are aiming to develop and validate a prototype over
the next few months to stay on track for patent filing later this year.
 
comments
Spray system must deliver ~45 inches vertical reach with consistent
coverage.
Nozzle should provide 360° dispersion at 45–60° angle.
Actuator integrated into housing, not a standard push-button.
