RE: Summary of Terms for a Proposed Investment to Develop and Manufacture the invention currently named Dumpster Diver
DATE: February 10, 2025
The purpose of this term sheet is to lay out the roles, responsibilities, compensation and other salient points of the relationship between Mr. Jacob Bevier (the “Patent Holder”) and Mr. John Braganini (the “Financier”) regarding the Financier’s proposed investment to develop, manufacture, market, sell and license technology to produce the invention currently called Dumpster Diver (the “Product”) in a joint venture with the Patent Holder. The Product is a self-cleaning trash dumpster pod. Hereinafter, the Patent Holder and Financier shall be known as the Parties and/or the Founders. 
Once the terms are agreed-upon by the Financier and the Patent Holder, details will be laid out in a long-form, shareholders/operating agreement (the “Agreement”) to be negotiated and produced after the full execution of this Memorandum. The terms of the agreement as proposed by Patent Holder are as follows:
COMPANY The Financier and Patent Holder will enter into a joint venture (“JV”) and create a company (the “Company”) specifically for the purpose of this JV. The entity will be structured either as (i) a corporation established under Internal Revenue Code Subchapter S or (ii) a limited liability company taxed as a partnership for Federal and State income tax purposes. 
 The domicile of the newly formed entity will be Georgia and not Michigan as originally proposed by the Financier. Upon research by the Patent Holder’s counsel, Georgia has the following benefits over Michigan for manufacturing:
Georgia is known for its business-friendly environment, with tax incentives and support for manufacturing;
Georgia has a strategic location in the Southeast, with access to major ports and transportation hubs; 
Georgia generally has lower labor costs than Michigan; and 
Georgia has access to skilled, non-union labor.
Furthermore, the Georgia personal income tax rate (5.39%) is only one percent higher than Michigan’s rate (4.25%). Accordingly, the domicile of the newly established company should be Georgia. 
Patent Holder and Financier can share the cost of creating/establishing the Company. The Financier and Patent Holder will own an equal share (50%) of the Company.
KEY PERSONS In addition to the Patent Holder and the Financier, other key roles within the JV will be filled by relatives of the Financier, Apollo Braganini and Sarah Braganini.
RESPONSIBILITIES Once the Company is created, the key persons will have the following roles, responsibilities and titles in the Company:
John Braganini will serve as the Managing Director (if the Company is structured as an S corporation) or Managing Member (if the Company is structured as an LLC). Specifically, John will serve as the business lead for the organization and is responsible for developing a clear direction for the Company;
Jacob Bevier will serve as the Chief Product Officer and will oversee the development, design strategy and launch of the Product;
Apollo Braganini will serve in an undetermined logistical/distribution role; 
Sarah Braganini will serve in an undetermined financial and administrative role;
The Company will hire outside counsel, engineers and other necessary contractors to help execute the mission and vision of the Company and specifically get the Product to market. 
Each Key Person shall enter into fulltime employment agreements with the Company and will receive a reasonable salary once the Company has positive net income.

CONDITIONS This Term Sheet is neither an offer nor a binding commitment to invest in the JV. Such a binding commitment will only be made pursuant to the execution of the Agreement mutually acceptable to all parties and only upon satisfaction of the following conditions:
Upon full execution of the Agreement, the Patent Holder shall license the patent to the Company for a nominal fee; 
In addition to 5% interest on the total amount of the Investment, the loan will be repaid by the first royalties that the Company collects for the license of the Product to distributors and/or the revenue collected from the sale of the Product to individual consumers; 
Both the Financier and Patent Holder shall enter into fulltime employment agreements with the Company for their aforementioned roles. Each will receive a reasonable salary once the Company has positive net income; and
The Financier has performed due diligence on this investment opportunity and is fully prepared to fund the Investment in the agreed-upon amounts and at the agreed-upon times. 
LICENSE OF PATENT The Patent Holder will license the Product’s patent to the Company for $1 per year, automatically renewed, for the first three (3) years following the execution of the Agreement. A license agreement will be prepared and executed in tandem with the Agreement.

MANAGEMENT For five (5) years following the full execution and closing of the Agreement, neither the Financier nor Patent Holder will be permitted to transfer their ownership interest in the Company, subject to any rights of first refusal established in the Agreement. 
BUYOUT Prior to the execution of the first sale and/or license of the Product to non-related third parties, the Founders shall have the option to initiate a buyout of each Party’s respective ownership interest in the Company upon the occurrence of any of the following events:
Failure to achieve key milestones as defined in the investment agreement (e.g., product development milestones, fundraising targets, regulatory approvals); 
Material breach of the Agreement by either Founder;
Gross negligence or willful misconduct by either Founder that materially harms the Company; or
Death or permanent disability of either Founder.
DEFAULT In case there is any breach of the terms laid out in the Agreement and other relevant transaction documents which is not remedied within thirty (30) business days from the date of the occurrence of the breach, the Financier and Patent Holder will be required to acquire the other Party’s interest in the Company. The acquisition mechanism shall be detailed in the Agreement.
INSURANCE In addition to general liability and other required business insurance policies, the Company shall also procure a director and officer liability insurance policy to protect the Company and its key management from liability in association with executing the mission, vision and strategy of the Company.
 The Company will also enter into indemnification agreements with key management that will indemnify each Key Person as permitted by applicable law.
NON-COMPETE The Financier, Patent Holder and Key Persons shall not directly or indirectly engage in any business activity with or in any way associate with a person or Company that directly or indirectly competes with the Company, so long as (i) they are owners or managers of the Company.
