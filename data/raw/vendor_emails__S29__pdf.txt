Additional positives from the testing:
Strong, consistent output
Foam structure behaved well through full discharge
Peppermint scent carried cleanly
Performance was stable and repeatable
The only meaningful limitation identified was spray geometry, not chemistry or performance.
Because the nozzle produces a flat fan pattern, coverage inside the bin was directional and
one-sided. ARE Labs specifically noted that a round nozzle producing a conical spray
pattern would significantly improve coverage and fully leverage the height and output
already being achieved.
From our standpoint, the difficult part — achieving height with acceptable foam behavior —
has been addressed. What remains feels like a distribution refinement rather than any
fundamental formulation change.
With that in mind, I wanted to ask about the best path forward:
Do you have access to alternative nozzles or actuators that would produce a conical
spray pattern, or a small variety we could evaluate?
How would you recommend we go about obtaining additional cans configured with
those options?
What would the next steps look like on your end to make that happen?
The testing gave us a lot of confidence, and it genuinely feels like we’re at the point where a
small adjustment could make a big difference.
