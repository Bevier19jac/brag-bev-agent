Subject: Testing Completed and ARE Labs results — next steps
 
Hi Jim,
I wanted to follow up now that ARE Labs has completed formal testing on the cans
your team provided. They returned the product with a short report, and the results
really confirm that we are extremely close.
One of the biggest open questions going into testing was whether the formulation
and overall design approach could achieve sufficient vertical spray height inside a 96-
gallon bin. Several groups we spoke with previously felt that target wasn’t realistic. The
testing showed otherwise — the spray consistently reached the interior lid surface,
which is a major milestone and a strong validation of the direction you took.
Additional positives from the testing:
Strong, consistent output
Foam structure behaved well through full discharge
Peppermint scent carried cleanly
Performance was stable and repeatable
The only meaningful limitation identified was spray geometry, not chemistry or
performance. Because the nozzle produces a flat fan pattern, coverage inside the bin
was directional and one-sided. ARE Labs specifically noted that a round nozzle
producing a conical spray pattern would significantly improve coverage and fully
leverage the height and output already being achieved.
From our standpoint, the difficult part — achieving height with acceptable foam
behavior — has been addressed. What remains feels like a distribution refinement
rather than any fundamental formulation change.
With that in mind, I wanted to ask about the best path forward:
