NON-DISCLOSURE AND CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT 
 
This NON-DISCLOSURE AND CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") dated 
as of the date executed below (the “Effective Date”), by and between Precision Valve Holdings 
Corporation (collectively with its parents and subsidiaries, “Precision”), and the undersigned entity 
(the “Company”). On a collective basis, Precision and the Company may be referred to as the 
“Parties” to this Agreement. 
 
RECITALS: 
 
WHEREAS, Precision and the Company wish to exchange certain data and other 
information of a confidential or proprietary nature, all for the purpose of exploring potential and/or 
existing business relationships, to the parties’ mutual benefit (the “Purpose”); and 
WHEREAS, for the purposes herein, either the Company or Precision, respectively 
(“Disclosing Party”) may disclose, from time to time, such data and information to Precision or 
the Company, respectively (“Receiving Party”) on a confidential basis; 
NOW THEREFORE, for and good and valuable consideration, the receipt and legal 
adequacy of which is hereby acknowledged, Precision and the Company agree as follows: 
 
1. Confidential Information . For purposes of this Agreement, the term “Confidential 
Information” shall include, but not be limited to, all information (whether or not in writing, 
whether or not specifically labeled or identified as “confidential” and regardless of the form 
or medium in which such information is embodied) of a private, secret, non- public or 
reasonably considered confidential nature (given the nature of the information or 
circumstances surrounding its disclosure) concerning Disclosing Party’s business, business 
relationships or financial affairs, including, without limitation, (i) non- public information 
about the Disclosing Party’s intellectual property, trade secrets, unpublished patent 
applications, data, methods of operation, manufacturing, products or services, regulatory 
compliance, processes, product technologies, developments, or plans, (ii) third- party 
information that Disclosing Party is obligated to keep confidential, and (iii) the nature, 
content and existence of this Agreement, the Purpose, and any discussions or negotiations 
between the Parties. The term “Confidential Information” also includes in formation that 
Receiving Party knows or has reason to know is confidential. For avoidance of doubt, 
information shall be deemed to be “disclosed” if Receiving Party accesses, receives or 
otherwise acquires such information in the course of fulfilling the Purpose, regardless of 
whether such disclosure was intended. 
 
2. Non-Disclosure of Confidential Information. The Receiving Party agrees that it will use 
the Confidential Information that it receives solely for the Receiving Party’s informational 
purposes and for no other purpose. The Receiving Party shall keep the Confidential 
Information strictly confidential and shall not disclose any of the Confidential Information 
to any person or entity or use any of the Confidential Information for any other purpose. 
3. L imitations of Confidential Information . For purposes of this Agreement, the term 
"Confidential Information" does not include any information that (a) was in the public 
domain at the time it was communicated to the Receiving Party ; (b) entered the public

All Confidential Information furnished to the Receiving Party by the Disclosing Party is

4. Term. This Agreement shall continue in full force and effect for a period of five (5 ) years 
as of the Effective Date (the “Term”) . However , the duties and obligations to protect 
Confidential Information disclosed under this Agreement during the Term shall survive 
any ter mination or expiration of this Agreement. Both Parties shall have the option to 
extend the Term upon such terms and conditions as both Parties may mutually agree. 
5. R eturn of Property and Confidential Information. Upon the request of the Disclosing Party 
at any time, Receiving Party agrees to immediately return to the Disclosing Party all of the 
Disclosing Party ’s Confidential Information within Receiving Party ’s possession or 
control, including but not limited to all originals and all copies of Confidential Information 
(in whatever form the same may exist, including documentary, electronic, audio, video or 
other), without retaining any copies or access to any C onfidential Information. The 
obligations contained in this Paragraph shall also apply to any property (including 
Confidential Information) which belongs to a third- party that was provided to the 
Receiving Party. Notwithstanding the foregoing, (a) the Receiving Party may retain in a 
secure place in its legal files one archival copy of the Confidential Information solely for 
verification purposes and (b) the Receiving Party shall not be required (i) to destroy or 
erase any general electronic archive or back -up tapes that are routinely kept by the 
Receiving Party in the ordinary course of business pursuant to records retention policies, 
(ii) to return, destroy or erase any documentation (A) subject to “litigation holds” on 
destruction of documents imposed by its or their counsel in connection with pending or 
threatened litigation or (B) which is the subject of a dispute regarding this Agreement, the 
Purpose or what was included in such Confidential Information or (iii) to return, destroy 
or erase any documentation required to be retained by reason of legal, accounting or 
regulatory purposes or requirements, with such retained copies to be kept confidential i n 
accordance with the terms hereof. 
6. P
ermitted Disclosure . In the event that the Receiving Party is requested or required (by 
deposition, interrogatory, request for documents, subpoena, civil investigative demand or 
similar legal, judicial or regulatory process or as otherwise required by applicable law or 
regulation) to disclose any of the Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall (a) to 
the extent practicable, provide the Disclosing Party with prompt written notice of such 
request or requirement, and (b) cooperate with the reasona ble requests of the Disclosing 
Party so that it may seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy. In the event that 
such protective order or other remedy is not obtained, or the Disclosing Party waives 
compliance with the terms and provisions hereof, the Receiving Party may disclose only 
that portion of the Confidential Information that , in the written opinion of the Receiving 
Party’s counsel, the Receiving Party is legally required to disclose. 
7. No License. Each of the parties acknowledges that the Confidential Information is the 
property of the Disclosing Party and the disclosure thereof to the Receiving Party does not

Each of the parties agrees in its capacity as a Receiving Party not to appropriate the

8. No Representation Regarding Confidential Information. The Receiving Party understands, 
acknowledges and agrees that the Disclosing Party is not making any representation or 
warranty hereunder, express or implied, as to the accuracy or completeness of the 
Confidential Information furnished to the Receiving Party , that any Confidential

nor assume any liability hereunder to the Receiving Party for any matter contained in or 
omitted from any of the Confidential Information. No license or other right in the 
Confidential Information is granted pursuant to this Agreement. 
9. R
emedies . Receiving Party agrees that money damages would not be a sufficient remedy 
(and is not readily calculable) for any breach of this Agreement and that the Disclosing 
Party will be irreparably harmed as a result of any such breach and that it shall be entitled 
to equitable relief, including, without limitation, injunctive relief (including, without 
limitation, the right to obtain a temporary and/or permanent injunction) and specific 
performance (without being required to obtain a bond or post other security or prove actual 
damages or irreparable harm), in the event of any breach or threatened breach of any of the 
provisions of this Agreement by the Receiving Party, in addition to all other rights and 
remedies available to the Disclosing Party, whether at law, in equity or otherwise relating 
to such breach. All the rights and remedies of the Disclosing Party hereunder shall be 
cumulative. 
10. Anti-corruption Regulations. The parties undertake (i) not to have taken and not to take 
any action that can be considered a violation of anti-corruption legislation in force, 
including, but not limited to, Decree No. 5,687/06 (United Nations Convention against 
Corruption), US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act, and, as applicable, the Inter-American 
Convention Against Corruption (IACAC Convention), the Convention on Combating 
Bribery of Foreign Public Officials in International Business Transactions (OECD 
Convention), and the UK Anti Bribery Act 2010, and to take all necessary measures to 
ensure full compliance therewith, and (ii) to perform any obligation hereunder with full 
compliance with the highest standard of ethical conduct. 
 
11. Choice of Law; Jurisdiction . This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in 
accordance with the laws of the State of South Carolina, without regard to the choice of 
law rules, or conflicts of principles of such laws. The parties unconditionally consent to 
the jurisdiction of the courts of the State of South Carolina located in Greenville County 
and the Federal Southern District of South Carolina with respect to any action, suit or 
proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement. The parties unconditionally and 
irrevocably waive any right to have a jury trial in any such action, suit or other proceeding.

otice . All notices, consents, directions, approvals, instructions, requests and other 
communications required or permitted by the terms of this Agreement shall be in writing 
and shall be sent to Precision and the Company on their respective addresses or facsimile

13. Assignment. This Agreemen t is not assignable without the prior written consent of 
Precision. Any attempted assignment without such consent shall be void ab initio and of 
no force or effect. This Agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of 
Precision and the Company hereto, and their respective successors and permitted assigns. 
14. S
everability . If any provision of this Agreement is held by a court of competent jurisdiction 
to be invalid, illegal or unenforceable, such invalidity, illegality or unenforceability shall 
not affect or render invalid, illegal or unenforceable any other provision of this Agreement 
and to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, such provision will be valid, legal and 
fully enforceable, and such invalid, illegal or unenforceable provision shall be amended in 
a manner that such provision will be valid, legal and fully enforceable. It is the intent of 
Precision and the Company that each and every provision of this Agreement shall be 
enforced to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law. 
15. E
ntire Agreement; Amendment. This Agreement sets forth the entire understanding and 
agreement between Precision and the Company with respect to the subject matter hereof 
and it supersedes all prior and/or contemporaneous understandings and agreements, 
whether written or oral, with respect to such subject matter, all of which are merged herein. 
This Agreement may not be modified, released, discharged, abandoned or otherwise 
amended, in whole or in part, except by a written instrument, executed by Precision and 
the Company. 
16. H
eadings . The headings contained in this Agreement are for reference purposes only and 
shall not affect in any way the meaning, construction or interpretation of this Agreement. 
17. C ounterparts . This Agreement may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of 
which when executed shall be deemed to be an original, but all of which, when together, 
shall constitute one and the same document. This Agreement may be executed by facsimile 
or electronic signature (including .pdf or similar image) which shall constitute a valid and 
binding signature for the purposes hereof.
