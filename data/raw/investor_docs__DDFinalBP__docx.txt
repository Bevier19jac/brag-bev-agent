The Self-Righting Automatic Dumpster Cleaner
Patent Pending: Application No. 63/723,626 (Confirmation No. 7443)
Executive Summary
The Self-Righting Automatic Dumpster Cleaner is a patent-pending, disposable, and eco-friendly cleaning innovation designed to simplify and revolutionize dumpster sanitation. The device leverages advanced delivery methods to release cleaning agents that effectively break down grime, grease, and odors. With its built-in self-righting technology, a pull-tab activation system, and a 5-second backup timer, this product ensures optimal coverage and reliable performance.
This versatile product targets both residential and commercial markets, addressing the growing need for cost-effective, hygienic, and efficient solutions to maintaining clean trash bins and dumpsters. By introducing this innovation to retail, online, and wholesale markets, the Self-Righting Automatic Dumpster Cleaner aims to redefine sanitation standards nationwide.

Product Description
How It Works
Activation Mechanism:
A pull-tab system initiates the release of cleaning agents via advanced delivery mechanisms.
Impact Trigger:
Cleaning action starts upon contact with the bottom of the bin or dumpster.
Backup Timer:
A built-in 5-second timer ensures reliable activation, even if the impact trigger is delayed.
Delivery Methods
Foaming Technology:
Releases a high-expansion foam that clings to surfaces, providing thorough coverage and cleaning action.
Aerosol Technology:
Propels a fine mist of cleaning agents to coat surfaces quickly and evenly, ensuring efficient sanitation.
Self-Righting Technology
The device remains upright during activation, ensuring maximum coverage and performance.
Eco-Friendly Composition
The biodegradable casing and non-toxic cleaning agents align with sustainability goals, minimizing environmental impact.

1. Define the Prototype’s Core Functions
The prototype should:
Deliver cleaning agents via aerosol and foaming technology options.
Activate upon pulling a pull-tab and include an impact trigger as the primary mechanism.
Self-right within the dumpster or bin for even cleaning coverage.
Operate with a 5-second backup timer to ensure fail-safe activation.
2. Prototype Dimensions and Casing
Dimensions:
Residential Model:
Diameter: 6 inches
Height: 8 inches
Weight: 0.5–0.7 lbs
Commercial Model:
Diameter: 12 inches
Height: 15 inches
Weight: 2–3 lbs
Casing Material:
Material: Biodegradable high-density polyethylene (HDPE) or polylactic acid (PLA) plastic.
Wall Thickness: 3 mm for residential, 5 mm for commercial.
Additional Features:
Textured grip near the pull-tab for ease of handling.
Label area for branding and usage instructions.
Color:
Green or neutral tones to emphasize the eco-friendly nature of the product.
3. Aerosol Mechanism
Core Components:
Propellant:
Nitrogen or compressed air as the eco-friendly propellant.
Pressure: 50–60 PSI for residential, 80–100 PSI for commercial.
Nozzle Design:
Multi-directional spray nozzle to ensure even dispersal.
Spray diameter: ~15 inches (residential) and ~30 inches (commercial).
Canister Material:
Aluminum or steel inner canister encased in the biodegradable housing.
4. Foaming Mechanism
Core Components:
Mixing Chamber:
Located within the housing, the chamber combines:
Foaming agents: Sodium lauryl sulfate (SLS) stabilized with xanthan gum.
Cleaning agents: Citric acid and sodium bicarbonate.
Volume: 250 mL (residential) and 1 L (commercial).
Dispersal Nozzle:
Produces high-expansion foam at a steady rate.
Nozzle diameter: 1 inch for residential, 2 inches for commercial.
Internal Compartment:
Separate compartments for dry cleaning agents and the foaming catalyst to prevent premature activation.
5. Activation Mechanism
Pull-Tab System:
Tab Design:
Made of durable yet biodegradable plastic.
Mechanically pulls an internal pin to release the cleaning agent or propellant.
Force Required:
Approximately 5–8 lbs of pull force for activation to prevent accidental triggering.
Impact Trigger:
Spring-Loaded Trigger:
Activates upon contact with the bottom of the bin.
Uses a steel spring coated with PLA to ensure biodegradability.
Sensitivity:
Designed to activate under a minimum force of 3 lbs.
6. Self-Righting Mechanism
Weighted Base:
Composed of a small steel weight encased in a biodegradable compartment.
Ensures the device remains upright in any orientation.
Stabilizing Design:
Center of gravity positioned low within the housing to enhance stability.
7. Backup Timer
Electronic Timer:
Simple circuit with a single-use battery embedded within the housing.
Timer duration: 5 seconds after activation.
Battery:
Single-use lithium battery designed to power only the timer mechanism.
8. Assembly Process
Steps:
Prepare Casing:
Mold biodegradable plastic housing according to the dimensions specified.
Integrate labeling and textured grip into the molding process.
Install Internal Components:
Assemble aerosol or foam chambers.
Insert propellant, cleaning agents, and stabilizers into designated compartments.
Install pull-tab, impact trigger, and timer.
Seal and Test:
Seal the housing with biodegradable adhesives.
Test activation mechanisms for functionality and reliability.
