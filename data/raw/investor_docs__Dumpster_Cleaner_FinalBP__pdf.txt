Business Plan for the Self-Righting Automatic Dumpster Cleaner 
Patent Pending: Application No. 63/723,626 (Confirmation No. 7443) 
 
Executive Summary 
The Self-Righting Automatic Dumpster Cleaner is a patent-pending, disposable, and eco-
friendly foaming cleaner designed to revolutionize dumpster sanitation. Unlike traditional 
methods, this innovative product requires no water for activation, instead utilizing a pull-
tab mechanism to initiate a chemical reaction that releases a high-performance cleaning 
foam. The device self-rights within the bin, ensuring full coverage, while a built-in impact 
trigger activates cleaning upon placement. A 5-second backup timer ensures fail-safe 
functionality. 
This product is designed for both residential and commercial markets, providing an 
affordable, hygienic, and convenient solution for cleaning trash bins and dumpsters. Our 
goal is to bring this innovative product to market through retail, wholesale, and online 
channels, securing nationwide distribution and making a significant impact on sanitation 
standards. 
 
Business Model 
1. Product Lines:

Product Description 
1. How It Works: 
• Activation Mechanism: A pull-tab initiates the chemical reaction inside the 
cleaner. 
• Impact Trigger: Cleaning starts upon contact with the bottom of the bin, 
ensuring seamless activation. 
• Backup Timer: A built-in 5-second timer serves as a failsafe, guaranteeing 
cleaning begins even if the impact trigger is delayed. 
2. Foaming Action: 
• Releases high-expansion foam that adheres to bin surfaces, breaking down 
grime, grease, and odors. 
• No water is required; the chemical reaction creates the necessary cleaning 
action. 
3. Self-Righting Technology: 
• Ensures the cleaner remains upright during activation, maximizing cleaning 
efficiency. 
4. Eco-Friendly Composition: 
• Biodegradable materials ensure minimal environmental impact. 
 
Chemical Composition and Specifications 
1. Foaming Agent: 
• Sodium lauryl sulfate (SLS) for high-expansion foam production. 
• Stabilized with natural thickeners such as xanthan gum.
