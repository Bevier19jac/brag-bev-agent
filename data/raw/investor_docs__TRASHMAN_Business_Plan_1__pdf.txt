Product Description 
How It Works 
1. Activation Mechanism: 
• Pull-Tab System: A durable tab mechanically activates the internal cleaning 
solution when pulled. 
• Impact Trigger: Activates the cleaning process upon contact with the dumpster 
floor. 
• Backup Timer: Ensures activation within 5 seconds of deployment, even if 
impact doesn’t occur. 
2. Self-Righting Technology: 
• Weighted base ensures that the device remains upright during activation for 
maximum spray coverage. 
3. Foaming Technology: 
• Combines a citric acid, SLS, and hydrogen peroxide reaction to create a high-
expansion foam. 
• Foam clings to walls, floors, and lids, dissolving grime and neutralizing odors. 
4. Eco-Friendly Composition: 
• Biodegradable materials align with environmental sustainability goals, 
decomposing within 6–12 months.

Foaming Technology 
Core Components 
1. Foaming Agent: 
• Sodium Lauryl Sulfate (SLS): Produces high-expansion foam. 
• Stabilized with xanthan gum for surface adhesion. 
2. Cleaning Agents: 
• Citric Acid: Breaks down grease and grime. 
• Sodium Bicarbonate: Neutralizes odors and enhances reaction. 
3. Catalyst: 
• Encapsulated Hydrogen Peroxide: Reacts with citric acid to release oxygen 
bubbles, expanding foam volume. 
How It Works 
1. Activation: 
• Pull-tab removes the internal barrier between cleaning agents and catalyst. 
• A spring-loaded mechanism punctures the hydrogen peroxide chamber, starting 
the reaction. 
2. Foam Generation: 
• Oxygen bubbles stabilize within the surfactant-rich solution, creating dense foam. 
• Foam expands 20x–30x, adhering to vertical surfaces for 5–7 minutes. 
3. Cleaning: 
• Citric acid and sodium bicarbonate dissolve organic residues, grease, and grime. 
• Deodorizing agents neutralize odors, leaving a fresh scent.
