The Self-Righting Automatic Dumpster Cleaner 
Patent Pending: Application No. 63/723,626 (Confirmation No. 7443) 
Executive Summary 
The Self-Righting Automatic Dumpster Cleaner is a patent-pending, disposable, and eco-friendly innovation designed to transform dumpster and trash bin sanitation. This cutting-edge device utilizes advanced delivery methods to deploy high-expansion cleaning agents that effectively dissolve grime, grease, and neutralize odors, ensuring thorough and efficient cleaning.
Equipped with self-righting technology, a user-friendly pull-tab activation system, and a 5-second backup timer, the device guarantees reliable performance and comprehensive coverage with every use. Its versatile design makes it ideal for both residential and commercial applications, meeting the increasing demand for cost-effective, hygienic, and sustainable cleaning solutions.
By strategically positioning the Self-Righting Automatic Dumpster Cleaner across retail, online, and wholesale markets, this product sets a new standard in sanitation, providing households, businesses, and municipalities with an innovative tool to maintain cleaner, healthier environments.
