Dumpster Diver Cleaning Solution Recipes
Objective: The Dumpster Diver product is a trash can cleaning device with an aerosol fogger being an integral piece of the puzzle. My vision is a high expansion foaming cleaner with the intent to effectively clean, disinfect, deodorize, and deter animals from outdoor dumpsters and trash bins. The product should leave minimal residue, provide long-lasting odor control, and meet the needs of residential users and potentially commercial users in the near future. Below are five proposed formulations for the chemist’s evaluation and input.
Recipe 1: Citrus Cleaner
Ingredients:
Water (Distilled): 70%
Decyl Glucoside (Plant-Based Surfactant): 15%
Citric Acid: 5%
Ethanol (Food-Grade or Denatured): 5%
Lemon Essential Oil: 3%
Sodium Citrate (Chelating Agent): 2%
Purpose:
Disinfection: Citric acid and ethanol kill bacteria and germs.
Deodorizing: Lemon essential oil neutralizes odors and leaves a fresh citrus scent.
Animal Deterrent: Citrus scents are natural repellents for many animals.
Recipe 2: Heavy-Duty Degreaser
Ingredients:
Water (Distilled): 60%
Sodium Lauryl Sulfate (SLS): 15%
Ethanol: 10%
Citric Acid: 5%
Sodium Citrate: 5%
Tea Tree Oil (Optional): 5%
Purpose:
Disinfection: Tea tree oil and ethanol provide antimicrobial properties.
Deodorizing: Citric acid and sodium citrate combat foul odors.
Deep Cleaning: SLS cuts through grease and grime effectively.
Animal Deterrent: Tea tree oil scent can deter certain pests.
Recipe 3: Bio-Enzymatic Odor Neutralizer
Ingredients:
Water (Distilled): 65%
Decyl Glucoside (Surfactant): 10%
Protease Enzymes: 10%
Lipase Enzymes: 5%
Bacillus Strain Microbes: 5%
Sodium Citrate: 5%
Purpose:
Disinfection: Enzymes break down organic waste and microbial contaminants.
Deodorizing: Microbes neutralize odors by degrading waste.
Animal Deterrent: Creates an environment less attractive to scavenging animals.
Recipe 4: Natural Vinegar-Based Cleaner
Ingredients:
Water (Distilled): 70%
Vinegar Powder (Acetic Acid): 10%
Sodium Lauryl Sulfate (SLS): 10%
Sodium Bicarbonate: 5%
Orange Peel Oil (d-Limonene): 5%
Purpose:
Disinfection: Acetic acid and d-Limonene kill bacteria and germs effectively.
Deodorizing: Orange oil provides a pleasant, long-lasting citrus scent.
Animal Deterrent: Orange oil is known to repel pests and animals.
Recipe 5: Sanitizing Cleaner with Oxygen Boost
Ingredients:
Water (Distilled): 60%
Sodium Percarbonate (Oxygen Bleach): 10%
Nonionic Surfactant (e.g., Polysorbate 20): 10%
Citric Acid: 5%
Sodium Gluconate: 5%
Ethanol: 10%
Purpose:
Disinfection: Oxygen bleach and ethanol provide strong sanitizing effects.
Deodorizing: Citric acid and sodium gluconate combat odors effectively.
Deep Cleaning: Surfactants ensure grease and grime removal.
Animal Deterrent: Minimal residual odors discourage pests from returning.
Additional Notes for the Chemist:
Aerosol Compatibility: The formulations should remain low-viscosity and suitable for atomization with aerosol foggers.
Residue: The solution should leave minimal or no residue after application.
Safety: The product must be safe for humans, animals, and the environment while maintaining efficacy.
Testing Requirements: Conduct tests for atomization quality, cleaning efficacy, and animal deterrent effectiveness.
Please provide feedback on these formulations, suggest modifications, or propose alternate ingredients to optimize the product for its intended purpose.
