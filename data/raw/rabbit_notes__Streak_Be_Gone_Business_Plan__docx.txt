Streak Be Gone Business Plan
1. Executive Summary
Product Name: Streak Be Gone
 Product Description: A flushable, pre-formed material that floats on top of the water in a toilet bowl to cradle waste and prevent streaks, reducing the need for frequent cleaning.
 Mission Statement: To provide a convenient, eco-friendly solution for maintaining cleaner toilets without chemicals or extra cleaning effort.
2. Market Research
Target Market:
 - Homeowners & Renters: Individuals looking for an easy solution to maintain a cleaner, streak-free toilet.
 - Businesses & Offices: Restaurants, retail locations, and office buildings where cleanliness is essential.
 - Eco-Conscious Consumers: Those who prefer biodegradable, eco-friendly products over traditional chemical-based cleaners.

 Current Competition:
 - Traditional Toilet Cleaners: Toilet cleaning tablets, sprays, and scrubbers that are used to clean after each use.
 - Flushable Toilet Wipes and Toilet Paper: Existing flushable products that don't address streak prevention directly.

 Unique Selling Proposition (USP):
 - A simple, environmentally friendly, and effective way to prevent streaks in the toilet bowl without additional chemicals or harsh cleaning solutions. It’s disposable and flushable.
3. Product Development
Material Options:
 - Biodegradable, Flushable Materials: Bamboo fiber, plant-based polymers, or other eco-friendly materials that can handle waste while being safe to flush.
 - Strength & Durability: The material must float and remain intact during use, but dissolve quickly after flushing.

 Design Features:
 - Shape & Size: Pre-formed to fit the typical toilet bowl. Designed to float and stay in place without sinking or breaking apart before use.
 - Easy to Use: A simple “drop-and-go” design—users just place it on the surface of the water before use.
 - Dissolution: It dissolves quickly after flushing, leaving no residue in the toilet.

 Prototype Development:
 - Prototype materials and shapes will need testing to ensure they can cradle waste effectively and dissolve without leaving any remnants.
4. Marketing Strategy
Branding:
 - Logo & Tagline: "Streak Be Gone" with a logo that conveys cleanliness and simplicity. Tagline could be “Flush Away Streaks, Keep the Shine.”
 - Emphasize the eco-friendly nature of the product through all branding materials.

 Distribution Channels:
 - Retail Partnerships: Target large retailers like Walmart, Target, and Home Depot to stock the product in cleaning aisles.
 - Online Sales: Sell through e-commerce platforms like Amazon and your own website.
 - Subscription Service: Offer customers a subscription option to receive regular shipments of the product.

 Advertising:
 - Social Media Campaigns: Focus on eco-conscious communities and cleaning enthusiasts.
 - Influencer Marketing: Collaborate with home improvement or eco-friendly influencers to spread awareness.
 - In-store Promotions: Sample giveaways at retailers to encourage people to try the product.
 
 Public Relations:
 - Highlight the eco-friendly benefits of the product in media outlets focused on home care, sustainability, and new inventions.
5. Manufacturing Plan
Suppliers:
 - Source biodegradable, flushable materials from trusted suppliers with a focus on sustainability.
 - Partner with manufacturers who have experience in producing disposable products on a large scale.

 Production Costs:
 - Estimate production costs based on raw material costs, manufacturing processes, and packaging.
 - Consider packaging the product in eco-friendly, recyclable materials to maintain alignment with the brand’s environmentally friendly positioning.

 Packaging:
 - Ensure the packaging is minimal yet informative, emphasizing the eco-friendly nature of both the product and packaging.
 - Ensure that the product is easy to store and use for consumers.
6. Financial Projections
Startup Costs:
 - Product prototyping.
 - Patent filing and legal fees.
 - Initial marketing budget (advertising, influencer partnerships).
 - Manufacturing setup (material sourcing, initial production run).

 Revenue Streams:
 - Direct Sales: Through the website, subscription service, and online platforms.
 - Wholesale: Retail sales to stores, both national and local.

 Pricing Strategy:
 - Price competitively with other toilet cleaning products, but also highlight the added value of streak prevention and eco-friendliness.
 - Offer discounts for bulk purchases or subscription services.

 Projected Revenue:
 - Year 1: Project moderate growth as the product gains recognition and retail distribution.
 - Year 2 and beyond: Expand reach through retail, online channels, and subscription services.
7. Funding Requirements
Initial Investment Needed:
 - Estimate initial funding needs to cover prototyping, patenting, initial manufacturing runs, marketing, and website development.
 - Consider looking into $150,000 - $200,000 in startup funds for the first year.

 Funding Sources:
 - Personal savings, crowdfunding (Kickstarter or Indiegogo), or seeking investment from angel investors or venture capitalists interested in eco-friendly products.
8. Exit Strategy
Acquisition Potential:
 - Larger home goods companies or toilet product manufacturers could acquire the product if it becomes successful.
 - Potential for licensing the patent to bigger cleaning companies if the product gains traction.

 Licensing Opportunities:
 - License the product to national brands that may want to add this innovation to their portfolio, generating passive income from royalties.
