Business Plan for the Self-Righting Automatic Dumpster Cleaner
Patent Pending: Application No. 63/723,626 (Confirmation No. 7443)
Executive Summary
The Self-Righting Automatic Dumpster Cleaner is a patent-pending, disposable, and eco-friendly foaming cleaner designed to revolutionize dumpster sanitation. Unlike traditional methods, this innovative product requires no water for activation, instead utilizing a pull-tab mechanism to initiate a chemical reaction that releases a high-performance cleaning foam. The device self-rights within the bin, ensuring full coverage, while a built-in impact trigger activates cleaning upon placement. A 5-second backup timer ensures fail-safe functionality.
This product is designed for both residential and commercial markets, providing an affordable, hygienic, and convenient solution for cleaning trash bins and dumpsters. Our goal is to bring this innovative product to market through retail, wholesale, and online channels, securing nationwide distribution and making a significant impact on sanitation standards.

Product Description
How It Works:
Activation Mechanism: A pull-tab initiates the chemical reaction inside the cleaner.
Impact Trigger: Cleaning starts upon contact with the bottom of the bin, ensuring seamless activation.
Backup Timer: A built-in 5-second timer serves as a failsafe, guaranteeing cleaning begins even if the impact trigger is delayed.
Foaming Action:
Releases high-expansion foam that adheres to bin surfaces, breaking down grime, grease, and odors.
No water is required; the chemical reaction creates the necessary cleaning action.
Self-Righting Technology:
Ensures the cleaner remains upright during activation, maximizing cleaning efficiency.
Eco-Friendly Composition:
Biodegradable materials ensure minimal environmental impact.
Chemical Composition and Specifications
Foaming Agent:
Sodium lauryl sulfate (SLS) for high-expansion foam production.
Stabilized with natural thickeners such as xanthan gum.
Cleaning Agents:
Citric acid for grease and grime breakdown.
Sodium bicarbonate to neutralize odors.
Activation System:
Encapsulated hydrogen peroxide for non-toxic oxidative cleaning.
Reacts with pre-installed solid organic acids upon tab pull, releasing foam.
Housing Material:
Durable, biodegradable plastic casing that decomposes within 6–12 months of disposal.
Safety Features:
Designed with non-toxic chemicals safe for residential and commercial use.
Childproof pull-tab to prevent accidental activation.
