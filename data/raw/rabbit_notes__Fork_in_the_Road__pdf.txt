## Section 5: Page 5
Path 1: DIY Formulas => MB Industries (BOV)
• Process: Source our own chemicals, mix/test small 1L 
batches, ship to MB Industries, Pennsylvania => MB fills 
into BOV cans => they send back units to test.
• Regulatory: No VOC concern; BOV is air-powered.
• Cost: Approximately $1,000 for chemicals and supplies 
for initial formulation batches. 
• BOV is usually 20–50% more expensive than a 
comparable traditional aerosol.
• On a per-unit basis, depending on volume:
o Traditional aerosol filling: $0.15–0.30 extra cost per 
can.
o BOV filling: $0.40–0.60 extra cost per can
• Pros: Full control of formulation, BOV = eco-friendly 
marketing edge.
• Cons: Slower, more bench work on my end, even with the 
DIY could be more costly in the long run.

Path 2: SprayProducts (Conventional Aerosol)
• Process: SprayProducts handles formulation + nozzle pairing + filling.
• Regulatory: VOC compliance not a problem (their expertise).
• Notes: They emphasized confidence, attitude, and alignment with our vision.
• Pros: Fastest to manufacturable solution, strong technical partner.
• Cons: Standard aerosol format (less eco-friendly than BOV), VOC compliance still applies.
