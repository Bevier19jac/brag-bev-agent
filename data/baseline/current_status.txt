Dumpster Diver is currently in active prototype development.

The project has progressed through early formulation and aerosol feasibility work. Recent work includes foaming cleanser formulation development, aerosol delivery feasibility testing, VOC compliance evaluation, and spray coverage testing.

Spray Products has been assisting with aerosol-related work and provided sample aerosol cans for testing. Rabbit Product Development is now beginning the mechanical engineering phase. Rabbit recently received the sample cans from Spray Products and is starting CAD modeling to integrate the aerosol system into the product's self-righting structure and activation design. The first mechanical prototypes are expected to begin taking shape in CAD during this phase.

Several engineering details remain under evaluation: actuation mechanism, spray geometry, mechanical trigger design, packaging format, and manufacturing approach.

Dumpster Diver is a working concept; performance, safety, regulatory, and manufacturing plans are subject to ongoing work.
