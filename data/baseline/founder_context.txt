Jacob Bevier is the founder of Brag & Bev LLC and the originator of the Dumpster Diver concept.

He is a disabled U.S. Army veteran with a background in technology and cybersecurity.

The product idea originated from the everyday frustration of dirty residential trash bins and the unpleasant process required to clean them. Jacob has pursued the product through collaboration with product development, aerosol, formulation, and testing partners to determine a viable path to engineering, manufacturing, and commercialization.
