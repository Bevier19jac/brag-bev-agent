Brag & Bev LLC is a veteran-founded product development company created by Jacob Bevier.

The company exists to develop practical consumer products that solve real-world problems. Its first major product in development is Dumpster Diver, a self-righting trash bin cleaning concept intended to make residential trash bin cleaning easier and less unpleasant.

Brag & Bev is working to bring this consumer cleaning product to market through collaboration with mechanical engineering, aerosol formulation, and testing partners. The project remains in active development; technical and business details may continue to evolve as prototypes and formulations are refined.
