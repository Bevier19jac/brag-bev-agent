Key terms and working glossary for Brag & Bev / Dumpster Diver

Brag & Bev LLC
- A veteran-founded product development company focused on cleaning and consumer products.
- Founded by Jacob Bevier. Currently in active development of the Dumpster Diver concept.

Dumpster Diver
- A self-righting trash bin cleaning concept. Intended to be placed into a residential trash bin after garbage pickup, when the bin is mostly or fully empty.
- Designed to trigger an internal cleaning action (aerosol or foaming spray) to help coat interior bin walls and reduce manual scrubbing. Exact mechanism, timing, and formulation are still under development.

Self-righting
- A device that tends to orient itself into an upright or intended position after being dropped, knocked over, or jostled. For Dumpster Diver this helps make cleaning action and coverage more predictable.

Aerosol / foaming cleaning action
- Triggered release of cleaning material inside the bin. May take the form of aerosol sprays, foaming agents, or a combination. Exact ingredients, safety, and regulatory status must come from specific technical or regulatory documents.

Bin cleaning goal
- Reduce direct manual scrubbing and give users a way to periodically freshen or clean bins in between or after regular trash pickups.

These definitions are high-level and may be updated as the project progresses. Treat them as directional context rather than final specifications.
