Brag & Bev LLC – Veteran-founded product development company created by Jacob Bevier. Focus: practical consumer products that solve real-world problems. First major product in development: Dumpster Diver.

Dumpster Diver – Self-righting trash bin cleaning concept for residential use. Intended to be dropped into an empty bin after garbage pickup. Uses mechanical or impact-triggered aerosol or foaming action to coat interior bin surfaces and reduce manual scrubbing. Simple to use; disposable or single-use; self-righting; mechanically activated.

Self-righting – A device that orients itself into an upright or intended position after being dropped or jostled. For Dumpster Diver this supports predictable cleaning action and coverage inside the bin.

Aerosol / foaming cleaning action – Triggered release of cleaning material inside the bin. May be aerosol spray, foaming agent, or a combination. Formulation and regulatory details are under development.

Bin cleaning goal – Reduce direct manual scrubbing and give users a way to periodically clean or freshen bins between or after regular trash pickups.
