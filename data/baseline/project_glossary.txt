Key terms and working glossary for Brag & Bev / Dumpster Diver

Brag & Bev LLC
- A company focused on product development for cleaning and related consumer use cases.
- Currently in active development of the Dumpster Diver concept.

Dumpster Diver
- A working name for a self-righting trash bin cleaning concept.
- Intended to be placed into a residential trash bin after garbage pickup, when the bin is mostly or fully empty.
- Designed to trigger an internal cleaning action (for example, aerosol or foaming spray) to help coat interior bin walls and reduce the amount of manual scrubbing required by the user.
- The exact mechanism, timing, and formulation are still under development and may change as the design is refined.

Self-righting
- Refers to a device that tends to orient itself into an upright or intended position after being dropped, knocked over, or jostled.
- For Dumpster Diver this is a desired behavior so that the cleaning action is more predictable and coverage is more reliable.

Aerosol / foaming cleaning action
- The concept involves a triggered release of cleaning material inside the bin.
- This may take the form of aerosol sprays, foaming agents, or a combination, depending on future engineering and formulation decisions.
- Exact ingredients, safety details, and regulatory status are not defined here and must come from specific technical or regulatory documents.

Bin cleaning goal
- Reduce the amount of direct, manual scrubbing required to keep trash bins cleaner.
- Give users a way to periodically freshen or clean bins in between or after regular trash pickups.

These definitions are intentionally high-level and may be updated as the project progresses. When in doubt, the assistant should treat them as directional context rather than final, locked specifications.
