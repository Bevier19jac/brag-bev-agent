Problem Context: Residential Trash Bin Cleaning

Residential trash bins frequently accumulate food residue, liquids, bacteria, and odor. Over time this buildup can create strong smells, insect attraction, and unsanitary conditions.

Most homeowners rarely clean their trash bins because the process is unpleasant and inconvenient. Cleaning typically requires dragging the bin to a hose, spraying it out, applying chemicals, and manually scrubbing the interior surfaces.

Because this process is messy and time-consuming, bins often remain dirty for long periods.

Dumpster Diver was conceived as a simple drop-in solution that could help coat the interior surfaces of a trash bin with a cleaning formulation after garbage pickup, reducing the amount of manual scrubbing required.

The goal of the product is not to completely replace rinsing or occasional deep cleaning, but to make regular maintenance easier and less unpleasant for homeowners.
