How Dumpster Diver is intended to work:

1. Trash is collected and the bin is emptied.
2. The user drops Dumpster Diver into the empty bin.
3. The device self-rights into an upright or intended position.
4. A mechanical or impact-triggered aerosol or foaming cleaning action activates.
5. Cleaning foam or spray coats interior bin surfaces.
6. Residue and odor buildup are loosened.
7. The user may rinse the bin if desired.

The product is intended to be simple to use, disposable or single-use, self-righting, and mechanically activated. The goal is to reduce the need for hoses, scrubbing, and direct contact with waste residue. Exact actuation, spray geometry, and formulation details are still under development.
