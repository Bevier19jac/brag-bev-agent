What is a CAD model

A CAD (Computer-Aided Design) model is a 3D digital representation of a product or part, created using specialized software like AutoCAD, SolidWorks, or Fusion 360. These models are critical in product development because they allow engineers and designers to visualize, test, and refine the design before any physical prototype is built.

Why CAD Models are Important:
Visualization:

Helps you see what the final product will look like from all angles.

Precision & Measurements:

CAD software ensures accurate dimensions, which are essential for manufacturing components like the trigger mechanism and can interface.

Testing & Simulation:

Engineers can run virtual stress tests to see how the device performs under certain conditions (e.g., impact triggers, pressure on the nozzle).

Collaboration:

CAD files are shared with mechanical engineers, manufacturers, or partners like Rabbit Product Development so they can build prototypes or adjust the design.

Prototyping:

CAD models can be directly sent to 3D printers or CNC machines for creating physical prototypes.

For your trash cleaner project, a CAD model will allow Rabbit Product Development to:

Design the device housing and ensure it fits a standard aerosol can (350-500ml).

Integrate the impact-trigger mechanism and nozzle system for multi-directional spray.

Check alignment and performance of components before committing to costly manufacturing.

While the rectangle is selected:

Duplicate for the Bottom:

Move the duplicate rectangle to the bottom edge of the slide.

Align it perfectly with the bottom edge.

Step 3: Apply to All Slides
