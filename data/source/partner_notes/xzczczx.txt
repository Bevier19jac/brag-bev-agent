what does disperse HEC at room temp

Great question — let’s make it simple.

HEC = Hydroxyethyl Cellulose, a powder thickener. On your batch card, the step:

“Disperse HEC at room temp; allow full hydration 15–30 min before heating.”

means:

At room temperature (no heat yet):

Take the HEC powder (e.g., 2 g for a 1 L batch).

Sprinkle it slowly into your cool water (part of the main water portion).

Stir gently while sprinkling so it doesn’t clump. Think of it like adding cornstarch to water — if you dump it in all at once, you’ll get lumps.

Allow hydration:

Once dispersed, let it sit 15–30 minutes while stirring occasionally.

During this time the HEC absorbs water, swells, and fully dissolves into the liquid, creating viscosity.

Then you can heat to 70 °C for the rest of the process (adding SCI, glycerin, etc.). Heating too early makes HEC clump and never hydrate properly.

✅ Plain-English rewording of that step:
“Add the HEC powder slowly into the room-temperature water while mixing, stir until there are no dry lumps, then let the mixture sit 15–30 minutes so it fully thickens before heating to 70 °C.”

what s the deal with rasberry pi and banana pi etc and now i see the pi phone

Great question — the “Pi” ecosystem can be confusing because there are different families of devices that all play off the success of the original Raspberry Pi. Let me break it down:

🍓 Raspberry Pi (the original)
What it is: A line of single-board computers (SBCs) developed by the Raspberry Pi Foundation in the UK.

Goal: Affordable, educational computers to teach programming and electronics.

Examples:

Raspberry Pi 4 → small PC with HDMI, USB, Ethernet, GPIO pins.

Raspberry Pi Zero → tiny, ultra-cheap variant.

Ecosystem: Massive community support, tons of tutorials, accessories, hats (add-on boards), and operating systems (usually Linux).

🍌 Banana Pi
What it is: A family of SBCs from a Chinese company (SinoVoip).

Why it exists: Basically competitors / alternatives to Raspberry Pi.

Differences:

More powerful hardware in some models (extra RAM, faster CPUs).

Often focused on networking, storage servers (some models have SATA ports).

Smaller community support compared to Raspberry Pi.

Compatibility: Many are partially compatible with Raspberry Pi accessories and OS images, but not always 1:1.

📱 “Pi Phone” (sometimes called PiPhone)
What it is: Not a product from the Raspberry Pi Foundation — usually a DIY project or niche device.

Examples:

A hobbyist project where you combine a Raspberry Pi with a GSM/3G module, touchscreen, and battery to make a working cellphone.

Some companies (like Banana Pi) market their SBCs as being able to act like a phone or IoT device (“Pi Phone” branding).

Reality check: It’s more of a maker/DIY novelty than a mainstream iPhone/Android competitor. Usually bulky, limited battery life, and built for fun or experimentation.

🌍 Why so many “Pi” devices?
The Raspberry Pi’s success made “Pi” a buzzword = small, cheap, hackable computer.

Other manufacturers jumped on the name for recognition (Banana Pi, Orange Pi, Rock Pi, etc.).

Each tries to differentiate with slightly different hardware (more ports, faster processors, AI chips, etc.).

✅ Summary:

Raspberry Pi → the gold standard, huge support, good for learning & projects.

Banana Pi (and others) → competitors with different hardware tradeoffs, less community help.

Pi Phone → DIY experiments or niche devices, not really a polished product.

🪨 Rock Pi
Maker: A company called Radxa (based in China).

⚠️ Weaker community — fewer tutorials, less plug-and-play support.

Raspberry Pi = biggest community, beginner-friendly.

Banana Pi = often tuned for networking and storage.

To keep your Microsoft (Outlook), Google, and Apple calendars in sync, you’ve got a few reliable approaches. The “Pi” family of devices we discussed earlier doesn’t apply here, but the linking process is straightforward depending on which calendar you want to treat as the “master.”

🔗 Option 1: Add Google to Apple Calendar (iPhone/Mac)
On iPhone: Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Google.

Sign in with your Google account and toggle “Calendars” on.

On Mac: System Settings → Internet Accounts → Google.

Check “Calendars.”
→ Your Google calendars now appear in Apple Calendar, stay in sync automatically.

🔗 Option 2: Add Apple (iCloud) to Google Calendar
Log into iCloud.com → Calendar → Share the calendar → choose Public Calendar.

Copy the URL (starts with webcal://).

In Google Calendar: Other calendars → Add by URL → paste link.
⚠️ This makes it one-way sync (read-only in Google).

🔗 Option 3: Sync Microsoft/Outlook (MMT?) with Google/Apple
If by “MMT” you mean your Microsoft (Outlook/Exchange) calendar:

In Outlook desktop or web: look for Publish Calendar / ICS link.

Copy the ICS URL.

Import that into Google (Other Calendars → Add by URL).

Or into Apple Calendar (File → New Calendar Subscription → paste URL).

For two-way sync, the easiest way is to connect accounts directly on your phone or Outlook:

On iPhone: add both your Google and Microsoft accounts under Settings → Calendar → Accounts.

Outlook desktop: add both accounts, then you’ll see both calendars side by side.

⚖️ My Recommendation
If you want all calendars visible in one place, add Google + Microsoft into your Apple Calendar app (on phone or Mac). Apple Calendar can display all accounts at once.

If you want Google as the hub, add your Microsoft and iCloud feeds into Google Calendar (read-only for Apple).

👉 Do you want two-way syncing everywhere (so you can create/edit events in any app), or are you okay with one-way visibility (all in one view, but edits only in the original calendar)? That’ll decide the best method for you.

Cell 3 — Text extractors (PPTX/DOCX/PDF/TXT/CSV)
def extract_text_from_pptx(path: str) -> str:
    prs = Presentation(path)
    chunks = []
    for i, slide in enumerate(prs.slides, start=1):
        slide_text = []
        for shape in slide.shapes:
            if hasattr(shape, "text") and shape.text:
                slide_text.append(shape.text)
        if slide_text:
            chunks.append(f"[Slide {i}]\n" + "\n".join(slide_text))
    return "\n\n".join(chunks).strip()

def extract_text_from_docx(path: str) -> str:
    doc = Document(path)
    paras = [p.text for p in doc.paragraphs if p.text and p.text.strip()]
    return "\n".join(paras).strip()

def extract_text_from_pdf(path: str) -> str:
    reader = PdfReader(path)
    chunks = []
    for i, page in enumerate(reader.pages, start=1):
        txt = page.extract_text() or ""
        txt = txt.strip()
        if txt:
            chunks.append(f"[Page {i}]\n{txt}")
    return "\n\n".join(chunks).strip()

def extract_text_from_txt(path: str) -> str:
    return Path(path).read_text(errors="ignore").strip()

def extract_text_from_csv(path: str) -> str:
    df = pd.read_csv(path)
    # Keep it compact
    return df.head(200).to_csv(index=False)

def detect_filetype(filename: str) -> str:
    ext = Path(filename).suffix.lower()
    return ext.replace(".", "")

def extract_text(path: str) -> str:
    ext = Path(path).suffix.lower()
    if ext == ".pptx":
        return extract_text_from_pptx(path)
    if ext == ".docx":
        return extract_text_from_docx(path)
    if ext == ".pdf":
        return extract_text_from_pdf(path)
    if ext in [".txt", ".md"]:
        return extract_text_from_txt(path)
    if ext == ".csv":
        return extract_text_from_csv(path)
    # fallback: try to read as text
    try:
        return extract_text_from_txt(path)
    except:
        return ""
Cell 4 — DB helpers (CRUD)
def add_artifact(file_path: str, tags="", source="", notes=""):
    filename = Path(file_path).name
    filetype = detect_filetype(filename)
    text_content = extract_text(file_path)

    con = db()
    cur = con.cursor()
    cur.execute("""
        INSERT INTO artifacts (created_at, filename, filetype, tags, source, notes, text_content)
        VALUES (?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ?)
    """, (now_iso(), filename, filetype, tags, source, notes, text_content))
    con.commit()
    con.close()
    return f"✅ Ingested: {filename} ({filetype}) — extracted {len(text_content):,} chars"

def list_artifacts_df():
    con = db()
    df = pd.read_sql_query("""
        SELECT id, created_at, filename, filetype, tags, source, notes
        FROM artifacts
        ORDER BY id DESC
    """, con)
    con.close()
    return df

def get_artifact_text(artifact_id: int):
    con = db()
    cur = con.cursor()
    cur.execute("SELECT filename, text_content FROM artifacts WHERE id=?", (artifact_id,))
    row = cur.fetchone()
    con.close()
    if not row:
        return "", ""
    return row[0], row[1] or ""

def add_decision(d):
    con = db()
    cur = con.cursor()
    cur.execute("""
        INSERT INTO decisions
        (created_at, title, status, owner, due_date, context, options, decision, rationale, impact, dependencies, next_steps, linked_artifact_ids)
        VALUES (?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ?)
    """, (
        now_iso(), d["title"], d["status"], d["owner"], d["due_date"],
        d["context"], d["options"], d["decision"], d["rationale"], d["impact"],
        d["dependencies"], d["next_steps"], d["linked_artifact_ids"]
    ))
    con.commit()
    con.close()
    return "✅ Decision added"

def search_everything(query: str):
    q = (query or "").strip()
    if not q:
        return pd.DataFrame(), pd.DataFrame(), pd.DataFrame()

    con = db()

    a = pd.read_sql_query("""
        SELECT id, filename, filetype, tags, source, created_at
        FROM artifacts
        WHERE text_content LIKE ?
        ORDER BY id DESC
        LIMIT 50
    """, con, params=(f"%{q}%",))

    d = pd.read_sql_query("""
        SELECT id, title, status, owner, due_date
        FROM decisions
        WHERE title LIKE ? OR context LIKE ? OR rationale LIKE ? OR decision LIKE ?
        ORDER BY id DESC
        LIMIT 50
    """, con, params=(f"%{q}%", f"%{q}%", f"%{q}%", f"%{q}%"))

    m = pd.read_sql_query("""
        SELECT id, title, status, owner, due_date
        FROM milestones
        WHERE title LIKE ? OR description LIKE ? OR blockers LIKE ?
        ORDER BY id DESC
        LIMIT 50
    """, con, params=(f"%{q}%", f"%{q}%", f"%{q}%"))

    con.close()
    return a, d, m

def build_weekly_update():
    con = db()
    decisions = pd.read_sql_query("""
        SELECT id, title, status, owner, due_date
        FROM decisions
        ORDER BY id DESC
        LIMIT 20
    """, con)
    milestones = pd.read_sql_query("""
        SELECT id, title, status, owner, due_date
        FROM milestones
        ORDER BY id DESC
        LIMIT 20
    """, con)
    con.close()

    def fmt_row(r):
        due = f" (due {r['due_date']})" if r.get("due_date") else ""
        owner = f" — {r['owner']}" if r.get("owner") else ""
        return f"- {r['title']} [{r['status']}]{due}{owner}"

    lines = []
    lines.append("WEEKLY UPDATE (Draft)")
    lines.append("")
    lines.append("✅ Progress / Wins")
    for _, r in milestones[milestones["status"].isin(["Done","In Progress"])].head(8).iterrows():
        lines.append(fmt_row(r))
    lines.append("")
    lines.append("🧠 Decisions")
    for _, r in decisions.head(8).iterrows():
        lines.append(fmt_row(r))
    lines.append("")
    lines.append("⚠️ Blockers / Risks")
    # pull top blockers
    con = db()
    blk = pd.read_sql_query("""
        SELECT id, title, blockers
        FROM milestones
        WHERE blockers IS NOT NULL AND blockers != ''
        ORDER BY id DESC
        LIMIT 8
    """, con)
    con.close()
    if len(blk) == 0:
        lines.append("- None noted")
    else:
        for _, r in blk.iterrows():
            lines.append(f"- {r['title']}: {r['blockers']}")
    lines.append("")
    lines.append("➡️ Next 7 Days")
    # anything due soon
    lines.append("- (Add your top 3 priorities here)")
    lines.append("- ")
    lines.append("- ")

    return "\n".join(lines)

STATUSES = ["Proposed","In Review","Approved","In Progress","Blocked","Done","Dropped"]

def ui_ingest(files, tags, source, notes):
    if not files:
        return "Upload at least 1 file.", list_artifacts_df()
    msgs = []
    for f in files:
        # f is a tempfile-like object with .name
        src = f.name
        dst = DATA_DIR / Path(src).name
        os.replace(src, dst)
        msgs.append(add_artifact(str(dst), tags=tags, source=source, notes=notes))
    return "\n".join(msgs), list_artifacts_df()

def ui_show_artifact(artifact_id):
    try:
        aid = int(artifact_id)
    except:
        return "Enter a valid artifact ID.", ""
    filename, text = get_artifact_text(aid)
    preview = text[:8000] + ("\n\n... (truncated)" if len(text) > 8000 else "")
    return filename, preview

def ui_add_decision(title, status, owner, due_date, context, options, decision, rationale, impact, dependencies, next_steps, linked_artifact_ids):
    d = dict(
        title=title.strip(),
        status=status,
        owner=(owner or "").strip(),
        due_date=(due_date or "").strip(),
        context=(context or "").strip(),
        options=(options or "").strip(),
        decision=(decision or "").strip(),
        rationale=(rationale or "").strip(),
        impact=(impact or "").strip(),
        dependencies=(dependencies or "").strip(),
        next_steps=(next_steps or "").strip(),
        linked_artifact_ids=(linked_artifact_ids or "").strip(),
    )
    if not d["title"]:
        return "Title is required.", list_decisions_df()
    msg = add_decision(d)
    return msg, list_decisions_df()

def ui_add_milestone(title, status, owner, due_date, description, blockers, linked_decision_ids, linked_artifact_ids):
    m = dict(
        title=title.strip(),
        status=status,
        owner=(owner or "").strip(),
        due_date=(due_date or "").strip(),
        description=(description or "").strip(),
        blockers=(blockers or "").strip(),
        linked_decision_ids=(linked_decision_ids or "").strip(),
        linked_artifact_ids=(linked_artifact_ids or "").strip(),
    )
    if not m["title"]:
        return "Title is required.", list_milestones_df()
    msg = add_milestone(m)
    return msg, list_milestones_df()

def ui_search(q):
    a, d, m = search_everything(q)
    return a, d, m
